Sunken Garden

Sunken Garden is a wide open green space at the University of the Philippines Diliman campus. It is a peaceful place to take a walk, talk with friends, or simply enjoy a break from the busy city. The large lawn and surrounding trees create an inviting atmosphere for a relaxed afternoon.

I visited once for a hangout with friends and was surprised by how much I enjoyed it. Even though I usually spend most of my time online, the calm surroundings made it easy to put my phone down and appreciate being outside.

AddressU.P. Campus, Diliman, Quezon City, Metro Manila
Opening hoursOpen daily
Price rangeFree admission
Best time to visitLate afternoon or early evening
