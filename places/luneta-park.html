Luneta Park

Luneta Park, also known as Rizal Park, is a spacious historic park in the heart of Manila. I recommend it as an easy place to slow down, take a walk, and spend time outdoors while still being close to the city. The open grounds, monuments, gardens, and nearby museums give visitors several ways to explore at their own pace.

As a TUP student, I sometimes visit when I have free time. It is a comfortable go-to spot for both locals and tourists, whether you want a quiet break, a scenic walk, or a place to appreciate an important part of Philippine history.

Address Ermita, Manila, 1000 Metro Manila
Opening hours Open daily
Price range Free admission
Best time to visit Late afternoon, when the weather is cooler
