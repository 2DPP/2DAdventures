The Province of San Simon

San Simon is a municipality in Pampanga with open spaces and a slower pace than the city. I first visited because of a school filming project, but the area turned out to be more enjoyable than I expected. Its broad fields and quiet roads make it a refreshing place to see a different side of the province.

One of my favorite parts of the visit was riding bicycles around the area. San Simon is a good choice for visitors who want a simple outdoor trip, time with friends, and a chance to explore somewhere beyond the usual tourist stops.

AddressSan Simon, Pampanga, Philippines
Opening hoursOpen daily
Price rangeFree to explore; bring money for transport and food
Best time to visitMorning or late afternoon
